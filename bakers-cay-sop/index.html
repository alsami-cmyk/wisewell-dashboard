Steps for each machine

1

Unpack and place it next to the Keurig

  • Place the Nano on the counter to the right of the coffee machine, as in the photo, on a flat, stable surface with its drip tray in place.
  • Plug it straight into the wall outlet (grounded, 3-prong). No extension cord or power adapter.
  • Leave about 8 in (20 cm) of open space around the fan so it doesn't overheat.
  • A few drops of water inside a new unit are normal. Every unit is water-tested at the factory.
Hardware check: is the WiFi light already on? Right after plugging in, before you pair it, look at the WiFi icon on the touch panel. Is it already lit white? Check this on every unit; we want to know if it happens across the board.
Anything else (blinking, another colour)? Write it in the sheet's Comments.
2

Fill the tank (Inlet only)

The external tank has two sections:

  • Inlet (large): fill to the MAX line with room-temperature drinking water.
  • Outlet (small): leave it empty. It collects the wastewater the purifier returns.

Then fit the tank back into place. The Nano draws water from the Inlet automatically.

Never put water in the Outlet Fresh water in the Outlet stops the filtration from working properly. Don't overfill the Inlet past MAX either.
3

Power on and test cold, hot and ambient

Press Power. For each temperature: pick a cup size (8.5 fl oz / 250 ml saves water), pick the temperature, then press Dispense.

Hot water Leave the cup on the tray (don't lift it up to the nozzle) and keep hands clear. Right after the hot test, dispense a little cold water to cool the nozzle.
4

Record the MAC ID in the sheet

  1. Find the MAC ID on the label on the back of the machine.
  2. Open the tracking sheet. The MAC IDs are already listed: find this one and type the room number next to it.
  3. In Comments, note the WiFi light check from step 1, e.g. "WiFi light on at plug-in: Yes".
Tip Take a photo of the label first. To find the row fast, search the sheet for the last 4 characters. If the MAC ID isn't listed, double-check the label, then add it in a new row.

5

Connect the machine to WiFi

Pair it using the Wisewell app on your phone. The video shows the whole flow (19 seconds, no sound).

Select Nano+ (not Nano, not Model 1) On "Which Wisewell do you have?", tap Nano+, even though this is the Nano Tank. It's a temporary backend issue. The video picks Model 1; ignore that.
  1. Open the Wisewell app and tap Connect a new device.
  2. Choose Nano+, then tap Continue.
  3. Do what the Prepare your Nano+ screen says until the WiFi icon on the machine blinks. Tick Wi-Fi light is blinking and tap Next — my device is ready.
  4. Wait while the app finds the machine and connects it to WiFi. This can take a few minutes. If it asks for the network, use the hotel WiFi:
  5. When you see Your device is connected, pairing is done. You can rename the device on that screen. The room number (e.g. "Room 214") makes it easy to find in the control center.
Not pairing? Turn on Bluetooth on your phone and check it's on the hotel WiFi (2.4 GHz if there's a choice), then try again. Still stuck? Message Jose (see below).

Reference

Safety and care (from the guide)
  1. Do not move the unit while it is running. Empty the water tank before moving it.
  2. Do not rinse or spray the unit with water.
  3. Plug directly into its own grounded (3-prong) wall outlet on a 20A circuit. No extension cord or power adapter.
  4. Place the unit and the tray on a stable, flat surface.
  5. Hot water can splash. Leave the cup on the tray under the nozzle and keep hands away from the stream.
  6. The nozzle stays hot after dispensing hot water. Don't touch it; dispense a little cold water to cool it.
  7. Supervise children or anyone with disabilities using the unit.
  8. If the power cord is damaged, stop using the unit. Only the manufacturer or a qualified technician should replace it.
  9. Use only when room and water temperature are between 41°F and 100°F (5–38°C).
  10. Use only drinking-quality tap water. Poor water shortens filter life.
  11. Fill only the Inlet, up to MAX. Never fill the Outlet, and don't overfill.
  12. Keep at least 8 in (20 cm) of open space around the fan.
  13. A few drops of water inside a new unit are normal (factory water test).
Refilling the tank later

When the Nano stops drawing water, the Inlet is below minimum. Empty both sections (Outlet water is not for drinking), refill only the Inlet to MAX, leave the Outlet empty, and reattach the tank. Never top up without emptying first.
